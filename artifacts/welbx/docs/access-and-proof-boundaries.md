# RTBX Travel Access and Proof Boundaries

**Version:** 0.6  
**Reviewed:** August 28, 2026

## Current evidence state

The RTBX Travel Partner Room is a **Working Proof** using **Simulation** evidence. It demonstrates interfaces, deterministic rules, governed workflow states and configurable models with synthetic inputs.

It is not an **Integrated** or **Production** customer deployment. No active page may imply that a named external system, property, partner or guest channel is currently connected unless dated evidence is added to the claims register.

## Canonical taxonomy

Use only: **Working Proof**, **Prototype**, **Simulation**, **Connector-ready**, **Integrated**, **Production**, and **Planned**.

- Connector-ready requires interface-contract and implementation evidence.
- Integrated requires evidence of a named external system exchanging data in an approved pilot or staging environment.
- Production requires evidence of an approved customer production deployment.

## Claims rules

- Synthetic KPI values, scenario outcomes, welfare results and financial estimates must be labelled illustrative, simulated, modelled or pilot-dependent.
- “Live” may describe future technical requirements or a genuinely current UI state, but must not be used as shorthand for interactive demonstration.
- AI language must name the assistance being provided. AI may assist interpretation, confidence, recommendations or drafting; named people retain approval, dispatch, welfare, safety, compensation and closure accountability.
- Demonstrations do not send guest communications, create staff tasks, activate partners, contact emergency services or write to customer systems.
- External proof links must be verified before they become primary CTAs. The internal Working Proof route is `/partner-room/guest-demo`.
