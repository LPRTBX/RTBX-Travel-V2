The moment most systems miss

VIP arrival logged
Priority room not released
Front desk capacity constrained

Protect loyalty value

Execution:

Host reassigned

VIP welcome and recovery flow activated

Outcome:

Queue pressure reduced
Room ready faster
Guest handled smoothly
Service failure avoided
Revenue and loyalty protected
