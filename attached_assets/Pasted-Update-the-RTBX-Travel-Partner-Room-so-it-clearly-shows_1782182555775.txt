Add a dedicated demo scenario for Holiday Parks & Outdoor Experiences.

Scenario:
A family arrives at a holiday park after a long drive. Weather has changed, the cabin is not ready, children are unsettled, and the guest is at risk of frustration before the stay has properly begun.

Demo flow:
Signal → Classify → Decide → Execute → Assure

Signal:
Late arrival, weather disruption, cabin readiness delay, family profile, low patience moment.

Classify:
High-risk welcome moment. Service recovery opportunity. Family support pathway.

Decide:
Trigger proactive arrival support, notify front desk, offer activity alternative, provide food/beverage or local partner option, escalate if cabin delay exceeds threshold.

Execute:
Guest receives simple message with support options. Staff receive recommended response. Operator dashboard logs the moment.

Assure:
Action completed, recovery offer logged, guest sentiment checked, delay reason recorded, value protected.

Proof of value:
- Escalation prevented
- Review risk reduced
- Staff response made consistent
- Guest experience recovered
- Partner/local experience opportunity created

Make this scenario feel like a live operating flow, not a static case study.
