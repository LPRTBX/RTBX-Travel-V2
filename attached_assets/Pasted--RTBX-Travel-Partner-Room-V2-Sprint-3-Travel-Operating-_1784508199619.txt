# RTBX Travel Partner Room V2

## Sprint 3 — Travel Operating Systems, Scenarios and Playbooks

You are working on the existing **RTBX Travel Partner Room V2** application.

Sprint 1 should have stabilised the repository, routes, branding, runtime and build integrity.

Sprint 2 should have aligned the Travel application to the shared RTBX architecture:

* RTBX Intelligence Engine
* Connect → Understand → Decide → Act → Learn
* Six intelligence layers
* Integration Hub
* Signal Registry
* Decision Spine
* Playbook Engine
* Prompt & Nudge Engine
* Central Comms OS
* Evidence Ledger
* Outcome Ledger
* Build & Configure
* Execution Centre

Do not repeat those sprints unless a blocker prevents Sprint 3 from being completed.

This sprint is focused on:

* Operating-system prioritisation
* Scenario quality
* Playbook completeness
* Accountability
* Escalation
* Evidence
* Outcome measurement
* Maturity transparency

This is not a rebuild.

---

# 1. Sprint objective

Strengthen RTBX Travel by ensuring that:

1. The site leads with three commercially meaningful operating systems
2. Safety and Guest Welfare operates as a cross-cutting control
3. Marketplace and Loyalty Activation is positioned as an expansion capability
4. Every visible scenario is linked to a complete governed playbook
5. Every scenario has an accountable role
6. Every scenario has explicit escalation logic
7. Every scenario has evidence and outcome requirements
8. Every scenario has an honest maturity status
9. Scenario, playbook and operating-system data are connected through shared IDs
10. Decorative or incomplete scenarios are removed from primary presentation

The core standard is:

> No scenario should appear in the Travel site unless RTBX can explain what triggered it, what decision was required, who was accountable, what action occurred, what evidence was captured and what outcome was measured.

# 4. Cross-cutting operating system

## Safety and Guest Welfare OS

Do not remove this system.

Position it as a cross-cutting governance and escalation capability supporting every Travel operating system.

It should provide:

* Human approval requirements
* Welfare escalation
* Security escalation
* High-risk decision controls
* Restricted communication pathways
* Evidence requirements
* Safeguarding controls
* Privacy boundaries
* Emergency or critical-response pathways
* Clear AI limitations

It may retain a dedicated page or section, but it should not compete visually with the three initial commercial operating-system wedges.

The site should explain:

> Safety and Guest Welfare controls sit across all Travel operating systems and become active whenever risk, distress, vulnerability or human-impact thresholds are detected.

# 5. Expansion operating system

## Marketplace and Loyalty Activation OS

Preserve the current architecture and scenarios where they are credible.

Position this as a later-stage expansion capability activated after RTBX has established:

* Trusted signals
* Operational adoption
* Customer permission
* Accurate context
* Reliable governance
* Proven response workflows
* Outcome measurement

It should not lead the first hotel pilot.

The page should state that marketplace or loyalty activation is only appropriate where:

* The guest has provided relevant permission
* The context is accurate
* The offer is appropriate
* Operational delivery is available
* The action does not interfere with safety, service recovery or guest trust
* Value can be attributed and measured

## 10.1 Repeat guest room not ready

### Primary operating system

Guest Experience OS

### Secondary operating system

Service Recovery and Staff Response OS

### Required trigger

A recognised repeat guest is approaching arrival or has arrived, and the assigned room is not ready within the configured readiness threshold.

### Required signals

* Reservation
* Arrival time
* Room status
* Guest profile
* Loyalty or repeat-guest status
* Housekeeping status
* Available alternatives
* Guest communication channel

### Governance

* Property room-allocation rules
* Guest privacy rules
* Upgrade authority
* Compensation limits
* Approval thresholds
* Communication standards

### Accountable role

Duty Manager or configured equivalent

### Required playbook

A complete pre-arrival or arrival recovery playbook.

### Required actions

At minimum:

1. Confirm signal accuracy
2. Assess room-readiness timing
3. Check alternative room options
4. Determine approval requirement
5. Assign operational owner
6. Prepare staff prompt
7. Prepare guest communication
8. Provide interim support or recovery
9. Confirm room readiness
10. Record evidence and outcome

### Escalation

Escalate when:

* Delay exceeds threshold
* No alternative room exists
* Premium or vulnerable guest status applies
* Guest sentiment deteriorates
* Recovery authority exceeds role permissions

### Outcomes

* Guest contacted before avoidable uncertainty
* Room or alternative provided
* Recovery completed
* Response time recorded
* Guest outcome recorded
* Repeat failure pattern identified

# 22. Maturity standards

Apply the approved maturity standard.

## Working Proof

A functioning interactive interface demonstrating the intended workflow.

## Prototype

A partially configured capability not connected to the full runtime.

## Simulation

A workflow using synthetic signals, decisions, communications, evidence or outcomes.

## Connector-ready

Only where:

* Interface contract exists
* Field mapping exists
* Authentication approach exists
* Test harness exists
* Error handling is defined

## Integrated

Only where data has been exchanged with a named external system.

## Production

Only where the capability is deployed with:

* Production authentication
* Monitoring
* Data controls
* Customer-approved governance
* Reliability controls
* Operational support

Every visible core scenario must have a maturity status.
