5. Readability standards

The site previously contained extensive very small typography.

Complete a full typography audit.

Required minimums

Use the existing design system while applying these general standards:

Content type	Target
Main body text	16px minimum
Supporting body text	14px minimum
Navigation	14px minimum
Buttons	14px minimum
Form controls	14px minimum
Card metadata	12px minimum
Status labels	12px minimum
Legal or technical notes	12px minimum

Do not use text below 12px for meaningful user-facing content.

Decorative micro-labels should be removed or increased where they carry meaning.

Line-height

Use approximately:

1.45–1.65 for body content
1.2–1.35 for headings
Line length

Avoid excessively wide paragraphs.

Aim for approximately:

55–80 characters per line for long-form content
Required changes
Replace page-specific inline font sizes with shared tokens where practical
Remove unreadable 8px, 9px and 10px text
Increase low-contrast supporting text
Increase spacing between headings and body content
Ensure status labels remain readable
Ensure tables do not require tiny text to fit
Allow content to wrap rather than shrink excessively
