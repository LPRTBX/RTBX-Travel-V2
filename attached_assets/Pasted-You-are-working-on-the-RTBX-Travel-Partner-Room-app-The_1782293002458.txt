Product Proof

Validation Lab

- Do not overcomplicate with backend logic.
- Use cards, tabs, toggles, steppers and state changes.
- Avoid long static text blocks.
- Each interactive module should help a partner, operator or funder understand what RTBX does.
- Make the demos feel like an operating system, not a brochure.

Final outcome:
