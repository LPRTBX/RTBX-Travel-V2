1. Sprint objective

Turn the RTBX Travel Partner Room into a commercially credible partner and pilot environment.

A prospective hotel operator, technology provider, implementation partner or strategic partner must be able to understand:

What RTBX Travel solves
Where RTBX sits within the existing Travel technology environment
What RTBX owns
What the customer owns
What a technology or deployment partner owns
What is required to run a pilot
What the pilot will prove
How deployment expands after the pilot
How commercial value may be created
What the correct next engagement step is

The primary commercial pathway must be:

Explore → Align → Configure → Pilot → Prove → Deploy → Expand

The site should lead with a controlled hotel or resort pilot rather than attempting to sell the entire Travel industry at once.

2. Core commercial proposition

The site must consistently communicate:

RTBX Travel connects existing hotel systems, operational signals, roles, policies and communications. It applies context and governance, activates the correct playbook, coordinates accountable action, captures evidence and measures outcomes.

RTBX Travel is not:

A replacement property management system
A replacement task-management platform
A generic chatbot
A standalone guest application
A new CRM
A generic dashboard
An ungoverned automated decision system

RTBX is the intelligence, governance and execution layer across the existing operating environment.
