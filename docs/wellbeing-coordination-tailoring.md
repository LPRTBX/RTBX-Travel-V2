# Future tailoring of the coordination pattern

These are design examples for later review, not implemented vertical features, integrations, live messaging, validated outcomes or approved deployment scope. This task changes Travel only. Each adaptation needs a domain owner, consent/privacy assessment, role and recipient mapping, escalation policy, systems access, integration testing and site readiness checks.

The Travel demonstration uses reported source facts → scripted suggestion → human review → verified capacity and agreed plan → approval → released communications → recipient acceptance → arrival/delivery receipts → timed follow-up. Changes reopen affected checks and approval while retaining history. Delivered support and reported benefit are different evidence.

| Future context | Possible tailoring | Boundaries requiring review |
|---|---|---|
| Enterprise | Worker explicitly requests practical support; supervisor coordinates workload cover and a named follow-up. | Private support details restricted to agreed recipients. No inferred mental state, diagnosis or employee risk score. Workplace rules and delegated authority must be agreed; this is not approved Enterprise roadmap scope. |
| Sport / Integrity | Participant asks for help; authorised support contact accepts the handover and records agreed assistance. | Explicit consent, safeguarding obligations, recipient boundaries and escalation responsibilities must be defined. General team views must not reveal the participant's private account. |
| Care | An authorised support team hands over an agreed practical-support task, confirms receipt and records delivery and follow-up. | Validate legal authority, care-team membership, consent/capacity and clinical escalation policies before adaptation. The Travel demonstration is not clinical decision support. |
| Protection | An approved action is coordinated across support, accounts and marketing, with only necessary task details delivered to each. | Separate commercial actions from private support information. Suppression/hold, authorisation and account changes need independently approved policy and integration checks. No automatic outreach or marketing based on inferred vulnerability. |
| Assure | Named issue owner confirms interim arrangements, coordinates repair and captures verification before closure. | Assignment is not completion. Repair and verification remain separate; unverified issues stay open. Determine qualified verifier, audit retention and approved system mappings before any live use. |

## Shared review questions

- Who owns the decision, response and next update?
- What did the person agree may be shared, with whom, and for which purpose?
- Is the receiving person actually available, and have they accepted?
- Which capacity checks, approvals and communications become invalid when the plan changes?
- What receipt proves delivery, and which outcome remains unconfirmed?
- What must remain open, private or subject to an authorised improvement review?

The browser's switchable fictional role views illustrate minimum-necessary communication. They are not production identity, authentication or authorisation. Live use would require server-enforced per-user permissions in addition to the existing Partner Room access boundary.
