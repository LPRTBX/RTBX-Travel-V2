---
name: Guest-channel terminology
description: The approved terminology and architectural placement for guest-facing experiences.
---

Guest-facing capability is configured, not branded. Use contextual terms such as **Guest Experience**, **Guest View**, **Guest Channel**, **Approved Guest Channel**, and **Guest-facing Experience** rather than introducing a replacement product name.

**Why:** RTBX Travel is the active vertical and RTBX Core is the shared engine. A named guest layer would imply a separate platform, channel brand, operating system, or architecture component.

**How to apply:** Place guest-facing delivery, communication approval, and guest-channel configuration beneath the **Execution and Communication Layer**. Preserve historical terminology only inside the archive; validation must exclude that archive and fail on active-source references.
