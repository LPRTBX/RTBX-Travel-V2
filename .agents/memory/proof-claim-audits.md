Proof-language cleanup must cover every active partner-reachable route and the data each route renders, not only landing, proof-summary or shared-banner copy. A page-level boundary should be reinforced by local labels for simulated actions, communications, evidence and outcomes.

**Why:** High-visibility copy can be corrected while linked scenario and demo routes still imply live integrations, completed welfare actions, dispatched communications or measured outcomes. Literal phrase bans also miss semantically equivalent claims.

**How to apply:** Trace links from active proof and demo directories, inspect rendered data sources, and test route-specific semantic boundaries: canonical maturity, synthetic inputs, local-only controls, modelled outcomes, rules-based current logic and named human accountability.

The public hotel-pressure story is an explanatory composite, not a new executable combined simulation. Hotels lead the explanation without excluding other Travel environments. Repeatability means reusing validated mappings, playbooks and configuration after systems access, integration testing and site readiness—not a blanket deployment-time promise.

**Why:** The owner requested a clear first-minute operational story grounded in existing working proof, while explicitly rejecting claims that the combined example runs today or that every integration/deployment is instant.

**How to apply:** Link the explanation to the existing room-delay, peak-pressure and portfolio proof; preserve their independent gates and unresolved evidence. Never turn public explanatory role emphasis into access control or expose private support details in broad role views.
